Completed Project

Automated Water Level Indicator and Controller

An Arduino-based smart water tank management system that automates water level monitoring and pump control. An ultrasonic sensor continuously measures the water level. The pump switches ON when the tank level is low and OFF when the tank reaches its maximum level.

This helps prevent water overflow, reduces water wastage, and removes the need for manual monitoring. Real-time feedback comes from an LCD display and LED indicators, so users can easily see the current water level and pump status.

How it works

  1. Measure HC-SR04 reads the water level
  2. Decide Arduino UNO compares it with low and max levels
  3. Control Relay switches the pump ON or OFF
  4. Display LCD and LEDs show level and pump status

Key components & technologies

  • Arduino UNO
  • HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor
  • Relay Module
  • 16×2 LCD Display
  • LED Indicators
  • Jumper Wires & Breadboard
  • Tinkercad (circuit simulation & prototyping)

Development · Group Project

ESP32-Based Smart White Cane with Speech-to-Braille

A group engineering project developing an assistive technology solution for visually impaired individuals. The proposed system uses an ESP32 microcontroller and a speech-processing system to convert speech into Braille output.

The Braille display uses a six-dot mechanism that raises and lowers individual dots to represent characters, aiming to display one word at a time. The objective is to improve accessibility and communication through an affordable electronic solution.
