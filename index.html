Development · Group Project

ESP32-Based Smart White Cane with Speech-to-Braille

A group engineering project developing an assistive technology solution for visually impaired individuals. The proposed system uses an ESP32 microcontroller and a speech-processing system to convert speech into Braille output.

The Braille display uses a six-dot mechanism that raises and lowers individual dots to represent characters, aiming to display one word at a time. The objective is to improve accessibility and communication through an affordable electronic solution.
